Four Books of Magic

Discover unforgettable adventures from J.K. Rowling's Harry Potter series.

Explore the books Meet the story

Harry Potter enters a world of friendship, courage and mystery. Follow him through four remarkable adventures filled with magic and wonder, as he discovers Hogwarts and confronts dangers that grow darker with every year.

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Harry learns that he is a wizard and begins his first year at Hogwarts. Alongside Ron and Hermione, he explores a school of spells while uncovering the secret of a powerful stone and confronting a familiar enemy.

Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets

A hidden chamber has been opened at Hogwarts, and strange attacks leave the school in fear. Harry, Ron and Hermione follow clues through the castle to discover the truth behind the chamber and its terrifying creature.

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

A dangerous prisoner has escaped from Azkaban and appears to be hunting Harry. As secrets about Sirius Black and Harry's parents come to light, Harry discovers that appearances can be deceptive and friendship can change a fate.

Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

Harry is unexpectedly entered into the dangerous Triwizard Tournament. He faces magical trials, tests his courage and begins to understand that Voldemort is preparing to return while the wizarding world remains divided.
